RELEASE: 0.9.0

Mario:
Can cancel jab 2 into any attack
FLUDD is best used as a recovery tool
If b is held during grounded up b, mario gets the first hit and cancels into his landing (good combo move)
Can wall jump out of up b

Dr.Mario:
Can cancel cape into attacks
Up b functions same as Mario, can wall jump out of it and cancel move while grounded if b is held (kill move unlike mario)

Luigi:
Can cancel jab 2 into attacks + specials
Can landing cancel fireballs
Can cancel grounded down b into attacks

Peach:
Down smash has set knockback on the final hit leaving opponents in a grounded stun allowing for followups
Jab 1 is fast enough to allow peach to follow up with other attacks

Daisy:
Aerial down b command grab has set knockback and can be used to combo into fair or side b
Side b is a self damaging kill move and combo ender

Bowser:
Built off P+ bowser (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials. Otherwise pretty standard with no quirks.

Geno:
If a is held/pressed during down angled F-tilt, the move automatically links into f smash
Geno has a crit mechanic where if you press A before your attack lands, it’s stronger
Press b to charge neutral b 1 level at a time, fully charged at level 3

Donkey Kong:
D-tilt guarantees trip on hitboxes close to DK
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Diddy kong:
Can cancel jab 2 into any attack
Neutral B can landing cancel

King K. Rool:
Tummy armor is removed on everything but side taunt
Side taunt can be canceled into attacks

Fox:
Shine has set knockback leaving opponents in grounded stun and can be canceled only on the ground by moving and crouching (can combo into itself/kill confirms)

Fox (Melee):
Same as fox, but can also still jump cancel his aerial shine

Falco:
Shine launchers opponents into falco but can only be canceled in the air with double jump and aerials
If you’re fast, falco can short hop double laser

Wolf:
Can cancel shine on the ground and in the air into attacks (example of good use is: drag down nair - shine - f smash, as a kill confirm)
Landing sweetspot side b doesn’t put him into freefall
Can landing cancel lasers

Yoshi:
Has double jump cancel
Has invincibility on shield drop and can still cancel his shield drop animation into anything just like everyone else, essentially giving him a very generous parry window
If you airdodge during double jump, you’ll stay locked on the x axis and still be able to drift (this is universal among all double jump cancel characters)

Link:
Can cancel jab 2 into any attack/special
F smash first hit trips opponents and can be jump canceled
Arrows are much stronger and can kill

Toon Link:
Can landing cancel aerial arrows
Aerial up b kills

Zelda:
Grounded neutral b can cancel into attacks
Can jump out of aerial side b

Sheik:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Ganondorf:
Opponents cannot tech grounded side b
Hold B after grabbing the opponent with aerial side b and it will launch away instead of up
If ganon still has his double jump during aerial side b, he can jump out of it
His up and down angled f tilts are his P+ up tilt and down angled f tilt

Ganon:
Pressing b gives cape while holding it throws a projectile
Down b can cancel into attacks
Side b is a kill move unlike ganondorf

Kirby:
Built off P+ kirby (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
The full jab combo ends in a dash attack automatically

Meta Knight:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters
Dair can be looped on grounded opponents
Neutral b is a kill move/combo ender

King Dedede:
Nearly all of his attacks have had their ranged greatly increased
Side b alternates between throwing gordos and waddle dees

Samus:
Down b can be canceled into aerials
Aerial side b missiles still landing cancel
Jab 1 is fast enough to combo into most other moves

Zero Suit Samus:
Dair trips grounded opponents and can be canceled into specials
Can double jump out of down b
Can landing cancel aerial neutral b
Can cancel jab 2 into any attack

Dark Samus:
Down b bombs stay active on the ground for a long duration and stun opponents
Aerial side b missiles landing cancel

Ness:
Down b has a multihit that can be canceled into attacks
Aerial side b can landing cancel
Has double jump cancel
If you airdodge during double jump, you’ll stay locked on the x axis and still be able to drift (this is universal among all double jump cancel characters)

Lucas:
Built off P+ Lucas (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
Down b is a kill move
Has double jump cancel
If you airdodge during double jump, you’ll stay locked on the x axis and still be able to drift (this is universal among all double jump cancel characters)

Pit:
Down b has a killing hitbox on startup
Grounded side b has a final hit, but can cancel into grab before it comes out
Aerial side b cancels on landing
Up b has super armor and extreme maneuverability

Pikachu:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters
Most tail attacks have more range

JigglyPuff:
Can cancel airdodge into aerials
Sing is cancelable much sooner (now good move for tech chase on plats)
Side b has a 2nd version that is slower + kills if b is held

Lucario:
Jab 2 can cancel into attacks and specials
F tilt can cancel into specials
If A is pressed after grabbing with grounded side b, lucario will cancel into f smash
New down b that can cancel into attacks

Squirtle:
Built off P+ squirtle (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
Can jump out of side b (ground and air)

Ivysaur:
Built off P+ ivysaur (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Charizard:
Built off P+ zard (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Mewtwo:
Built off P+ m2 (still heavily rebalanced/reworked) so has all the P+ specials.
You can still float after double jump if you hold jump
Has double jump cancel
If you airdodge during double jump, you’ll stay locked on the x axis and still be able to drift (this is universal among all double jump cancel characters)

Incineroar:
Side b can grab opponents off missed tech
Failed side b can combo into some attacks (jab, d tilt, f tilt)

Marth:
Essentially nothing quirky about marth. Basically plays like how you would expect marth to play.

Lucina:
Grounded down b is cancelable into attacks and has good invincibility frames
Aerial side b can cancel into attacks and double jump but has no invincibility

Ike:
Grounded side b can be jumped out of
Aerial side be can be double jumped out of and has no landing lag

Roy:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Melee Roy:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Ven:
Ice roy…
Can cancel counter into attacks
Can cancel side b up 3 into attacks
Side b 1 has set knockback and can combo into some attacks

Rob:
Side b reworked to be a multihit into a strong final hit
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Mr. Game & Watch:
Made 9’s more frequent and 1’s less frequent
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Isabelle:
Nothing very quirky, just a heavily rebalanced Isabelle from PMEX

Wario:
After grabbing someone with neutral b, you can cancel into attacks
Dair can be double jumped out of early

Little Mac:
Can cancel rolls into attacks
Down b is an unblockable that trips, it can also be canceled into attacks
F tilt 1st hit trips and can cancel into specials
Can double jump out of side b

Captain Falcon:
If A is held during side b, falcon will grab instead of doing the uppercut
If aerial side b connects, falcon gets his double jump back and can jump out of the end lag
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Snake:
Jab 2 and f tilt 1 can be canceled into specials
While holding an opponent in a grab, press B to stick them with/detonate C4
C4 detonate can be canceled into attacks

Sub-Zero:
Jab 2 and f tilt can be canceled into specials
Side b can be jump canceled both on ground and in air

Cloud:
Nothing very quirky, just a heavily rebalanced Cloud from REX

Sephiroth:
No glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Pac-Man:
Nothing very quirky, just a heavily rebalanced Pac-Man from REX

Sonic:
Down b has a multihit on startup that can cancel into attacks
Can cancel jab 2 into any attack

Knuckles:
Aerial neutral b is a dragdown with low landing lag, can combo into nicely into up tilt
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters

Shadow:
After neutral b homing attack lands, press b again for the followup kill move
Otherwise no glaring quirks, just has new moves, utility, and animations like most other characters
