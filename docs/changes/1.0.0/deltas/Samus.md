# Samus — 1.0.0

## Changes since 0.9.5

### Jab / Tilts

**Jab**
new animation from P+
more range and size

**Up Tilt**
new animation and properties

**Forward Tilt**
P+ animation, more range
bkb: 10 → 35
kbg: 100 → 80

**Down Tilt**
angle: 80 → 96

### Aerial Attacks

**Neutral Air**
buffed hitbox sizes
late hit damage: 6 → 9

**Up Air**
removed speed up during duration of move, still on endlag
multihit has 1 extra hit + lasts longer

**Forward Air**
buffed hitbox sizes
final hit angle: 361 → 55
sped up: 1.5 → 1.4x
removed slowdown on endlag

**Down Air**
startup sped up 2x
+3 active frames

### Smash Attacks

**Forward Smash**
given more range and now all angled variants share hitbox values
now a 2 hit multihit

### Special Moves

**Neutral B**
full charge shot stronger + buffed hitbox size
charges faster
can now charge in air

**Down B**
reworked explosion and samus can now do aerials out of down b
